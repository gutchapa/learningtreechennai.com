About Us

About Us

We are proud & happy to announce that Bodhana & Learning Tree Montessori has been conferred 'Excellence in Eductional Standards"  for the year 2024-25, Tamil Nadu by EduAwards India.  

Learning Tree Montessori School, founded in 2002, is located in Adyar, Chennai with a branch in OMR Kottivakam. Over the years Learning Tree has established itself as one of the premier Montessori schools in the city for children in the age group of 2 years to 12 years.

Learning Tree believes in the Montessori method to help each child spontaneously reach his or her full potential - intellectually, socially, and emotionally. Teaching values at Learning Tree are aimed towards imparting qualities of self-confidence, poise, a spirit of sharing, and exemplary social behavior.

Children have an opportunity to explore the world around them. They will also discover the power of words, find the magic of numbers, and see the significance of relationships, as well as explore subjects like science, art, and world culture.

Our Approach

The Montessori method, known for its child-centered approach, allows children to learn at their own pace and explore their interests in a supportive environment. At Learning Tree, this translates into opportunities for children to engage with the world around them, encouraging curiosity and a love for learning.

Key Areas of Focus
  • Language and Communication: Children discover the power of words, enhancing their ability to express themselves and communicate effectively.
  • Mathematics: They explore the magic of numbers, building a strong foundation in logical thinking and problem-solving.
  • Social Relationships: Through interactions with peers and teachers, children learn the significance of relationships, cooperation, and empathy.
  • Science, Art, and World Culture: Exposure to these subjects broadens their understanding of the world, fostering creativity, critical thinking, and cultural awareness.

Our Mission

By integrating these elements, Learning Tree aims to help each child reach their full potential, preparing them not just academically, but also as confident, compassionate, and socially responsible individuals.

The premises in Shastry Nagar, Adyar caters to the children in the age group of 18 months to 3 yrs (Toddler Community) & 3 yrs to 6 yrs (Primary Community).

The premises in OMR Kottivakam caters to the children in the age group of 6 yrs to 11 yrs (Montessori Elementary Community) & 12 yrs+ (Montessori Adolescent Pathway).

Over the years Learning Tree has been consistently ranked as among the top pre-schools in Chennai. In the school rankings released by Education World in December 2019, Learning Tree has maintained its ranking as Chennai's 4th best preschool for the third time in a row.
